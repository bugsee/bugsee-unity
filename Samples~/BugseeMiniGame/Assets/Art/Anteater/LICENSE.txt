Anteater character assets
========================

Generated/
  AnteaterMesh.obj / .mtl     — remeshed game mesh (~14k tris) used by the sample
  AnteaterMesh.source.glb     — remeshed intermediate from the Meshy export

Source: Meshy AI image-to-3D from Bugsee anteater logo artwork, converted from
Meshy's download to GLB, then remeshed (gltf-transform simplify) for mobile.
Original Meshy export had ~458k triangles, no textures, no skeleton, no clips.

Materials in-game are authored at runtime (coral/brown lit) because the export
had no albedo maps.

Animator Idle/Walk was not added: the mesh has no skin or animation clips.
Locomotion stays CharacterController-driven; procedural Path 3 remains the
fallback when Resources/Anteater/AnteaterMesh is missing.

Respect Meshy Terms of Service for the generated geometry. Bugsee logo PNG in
this folder remains Bugsee artwork for the spawn totem.
